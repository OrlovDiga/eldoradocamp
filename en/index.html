I want to go

What to expect

10 days of surf camp

We’ll ride the best spots of the south coast. Groups of up to 5 — the instructor keeps an eye on everyone. Never surfed? We’ll get you standing in the first days. Already have some experience? We’ll polish your cutbacks and bottom turns together.

The buzz

What if I told you that in 10 days you won’t just surf better — you’ll also meditate with a monk, release baby turtles into the ocean, dance by a huge bonfire on the beach under the moon, crack open a coconut, and maybe even climb a palm for it yourself, like the locals? Think that’s all? No way — temples, secret beaches, lighthouses, sunsets and long heart-to-heart evenings. And all of it caught by our videographer — trust me, it’s worth it.

Your crew

Thought you’d take home only memories and photos? Not so fast!

Heard how hard it is to make friends as an adult? Forget it. On the island everything seems to work differently. People come here to live to the full, so you’ll go home with some great new friends too. And at the very least you’ll have a couch to crash on abroad: people come to us from all over.

And who knows — maybe this is where you’ll find a business partner. Or love :)

Island adventure

Know Indiana Jones? We made him up! Seriously though: other camps usually take you to a safari, tea and the bridge. We go further. In 4 days we cross the island from south to north: a safari in Yala, swimming in waterfalls (one is secret, hardly anyone knows it), the mountain train through the tea hills, the Dambulla cave temple and sunrise over Sigiriya. The van, hotels, safari and tickets are already in the price — all you have to do is feel like the hero of a movie!

How a day goes

Morning

Ocean first

Morning: sit-ups, jogging, push-ups…

We start with breakfast and surfing, and three times per camp with yoga too. We learn in groups by level, so everyone rides their own wave.

Afternoon

Siesta and outings

After surfing we rest. Twice per camp there’s an afternoon talk and video review. On the other days we explore Sri Lanka or just chill.

Evening

Sunset and dinner

Barbecues, movies on the projector, dinners together or parties — every day ends differently. But almost always with a sunset in a new place: Weligama beach, the Peace Pagoda, the walls of Galle Fort.

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Arrival and getting to know each other

    If you’ve booked a transfer, we meet you at Colombo airport. We check in, fruit and drinks wait on the veranda. We show you the villa and give a short safety talk. You can walk to Unawatuna beach for a dip and the sunset. In the evening, the welcome BBQ: we get to know each other and open the camp.

  2. Day 2

    The first wave

    Morning yoga and the first lesson: theory on the sand, then the water. After the lesson we stay for a swim while Artyom films everyone with the drone. In the afternoon, a talk on waves, currents and safety, plus the first video review. Towards evening we drive to the Yatagala rock temple: a monk leads a short meditation, then we watch the sunset from the rock. After dinner we watch the video of the first session on the projector.

  3. Day 3

    Weligama and a quiz

    Morning yoga and the second lesson. Towards evening we take the boards out in Weligama bay: the first evening session. We watch the sunset right there on the beach, then dinner and a quiz.

  4. Day 4

    Turtles and the Peace Pagoda

    The third lesson in the morning. In the afternoon we go to the lagoon by Frog Rock in Dalawella and swim next to wild turtles. At sunset — the Peace Pagoda on Rumassala. Dinner on Unawatuna beach with live music.

  5. Day 5

    The bus to Galle

    The fourth lesson in the morning, a talk in the afternoon: we learn to read the forecast and go over the lesson videos. The evening session is optional today. We ride to Galle on the “crazy bus” with music and stops. Then the lighthouse, sunset on the fort walls, a walk around the fort and dinner together.

  6. Day 6

    Mirissa and techno

    The fifth lesson in the morning, and right after brunch we head to Mirissa: Parrot Rock, Secret Beach and Coconut Tree Hill, with snorkelling for whoever wants it. We watch the sunset at Secret Beach and have dinner with live music. Then techno at The Groove Garden in Midigama; anyone who’d rather not heads home early. Not in the mood for Mirissa? Stay at the villa and practise on your own.

  7. Day 7

    A slow day

    No lessons. Whoever wants to goes to Jungle Beach early in the morning to snorkel out to the boats. In the afternoon, Ember & Ice in Ahangama: sauna and ice baths. Then rest at the villa, a massage if you like. In the evening, dinner by a bonfire on Habaraduwa beach: at sunset we release baby turtles, and the grill has fish and seafood from Galle market.

  8. Day 8

    The debrief

    The last yoga of the camp in the morning and the sixth lesson. In the evening we look at how far everyone has come, then dinner and a movie on the projector.

  9. Day 9

    The last wave

    The last, seventh lesson — the final filming. After it, Ember & Ice for the second time: sauna and ice baths. The evening is free: pack your things, or go out for one last surf. We close the camp with a farewell BBQ and karaoke.

  10. Day 10

    See you soon

    The last brunch and packing. We leave before noon, an airport transfer if you want one. Those going on the tour have breakfast a bit earlier and get in the van.

Villa & rooms

After the ocean you come back not to some random hotel but to our villa in the jungle near Unawatuna. We create the vibe and take care of the service ourselves. This is where we have breakfast after surfing, yoga and evening hangouts, and in the mornings birds sing and monkeys jump around outside the window.

The villa has Wi‑Fi, a workspace and a projector for surf reviews. Each of the 10 rooms has its own terrace, bathroom and air conditioning. Stay alone, as a couple or in a shared room. The spot is 9 minutes away, and a tuk-tuk is on call at the villa.

Island tour

After the camp you can head inland: safari, waterfalls, the hill-country train, tea and Sigiriya at sunrise.

Day 13, sunrise, Pidurangala

Sigiriya

Fifteen hundred years ago a king killed his father, seized the throne and hid from his brother on top of this rock. We watch the sunrise from the neighbouring rock, where you see it whole. After breakfast we pay the king a visit: the way in is guarded by giant stone lion paws.

A thrilling rendezvous

We leave the camp on the morning of day ten with all our things and don’t come back. That evening — a safari in Yala, and the next day you’re up in the hills, where nights drop to 10 °C. Your suitcase rides in the van, and on the last day we drop you right at your flight.

The route day by day

  1. Day 10

    Safari in Yala

    Instead of the airport we head east to Tissamaharama. We check in and sit out the heat. Towards evening we set off on safari in Yala in two jeeps. We spend the night there, in Tissamaharama.

  2. Day 11

    Waterfalls and the mountain train

    In the morning we drive to Diyaluma falls: a 30–45 minute climb, and you can swim in the lower pool by the black rock. Then Ellewala: a bathing spot and shade. Lunch in Ella, the Nine Arch Bridge and Ravana falls. In the afternoon we board the train to Nanu Oya: 2 hours 15 minutes along the most beautiful stretch of the hill line. Sit on the left-hand side, your luggage goes with the driver. We reach Nuwara Eliya in the evening. Nights here are 10–12 °C, so get your warm clothes out in advance.

  3. Day 12

    Tea and Dambulla

    We start at the tea plantations: how tea grows and how it’s picked. Then the factory and a tasting. Next, the road north, with Lover’s Leap and Ramboda falls on the way. Towards evening, the Dambulla cave temple: five caves, 150 Buddha statues and painted ceilings. We reach Sigiriya in the evening, have an early dinner and go to bed: tomorrow we’re up before dawn.

  4. Day 13

    Sigiriya at sunrise

    We get up in the dark and climb Pidurangala for half an hour: at sunrise you see the whole Lion Rock from the top. After breakfast, Sigiriya itself: the frescoes, the mirror wall, the palace on top. Feel free to bring food: there are loads of monkeys at the summit. Lunch, then about four hours to the airport — for an evening or night flight.

FAQ

Can I come with no experience?

Yes. The first lesson starts with theory on the sand, then the water. The instructor is with you at all 7 lessons, and the video reviews show what to fix.

I’m travelling alone — what should I pick?

Going solo — awesome! Lots of people come to surf camp on their own, but you won’t be alone for long :) We get to know each other at the welcome BBQ on the very first evening, and after 10 days saying goodbye is hard. Want more company — take a shared room; need some quiet — a private room.

How do I pay?

A 20% deposit holds your spot, the rest is paid on arrival. You can pay by card in rubles, euros or dollars.

Do I need a visa?

Yes, but it’s free. Since 25 May 2026 citizens of 40 countries, including the UK, the US, Germany, Australia and Canada, get a free 30-day tourist visa (ETA); apply online at eta.gov.lk. If you need help with it, message us.

Do I need insurance?

Not for entry: the visa requirements don’t include a policy. But surfing is an active sport, so get a policy that covers surfing and medical treatment.

Where do I fly to?

Colombo, Bandaranaike airport (CMB). We’ll help you sort out the rest, just message us.

What about food?

Coffee and fruit before the lesson, brunches at the villa, two BBQs and the bonfire grill are included. Lunches and dinners outside the villa and dinners at the villa to order are on you.

What should I pack?

Light clothes, sunscreen, flip-flops. Boards and rash guards are ours. If you’re joining the tour, bring a sweater: nights in Nuwara Eliya are 10–12 °C.

How much money should I bring?

You pay the remaining 80% for the camp on arrival. On top of that: lunches and dinners outside the villa, dinners at the villa to order, the transfer, massage, the club, padel and billiards. On the tour food comes to about $12–18 a day.
